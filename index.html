Fed on live feeds from day one — that's why hatchery fish thrive.

GROWN, NOT CAUGHT

Hardier by upbringing

Tank-bred fish have never seen a cyanide bottle or a cargo hold. They arrive already adjusted to aquarium life and already eating — the two things that kill wild-caught fish.

FED LIKE THE OCEAN FEEDS

Live prey, real instincts

Pods and rotifers trigger natural hunting behaviour. Colours deepen, breeding pairs condition faster, and "impossible" feeders like mandarins simply start eating.

CULTURED IN CHENNAI

Fresh beats imported

Our cultures never cross a border or sit in transit for a week. What reaches your tank is alive, dense, and hours — not weeks — from harvest.
